Reviews a D&T NEA portfolio and tells you what to fix, in order of what is costing you marks.

**To use it:**

**Windows** — download `NEA-Coach-windows.zip`, unzip it, then double-click
`Start NEA Coach.bat` inside the folder it creates.

**Mac** — download `NEA-Coach-mac.zip`, unzip it, then double-click
`Start NEA Coach.command` inside the folder it creates.
(The first run downloads Node.js automatically; after that it opens instantly.)

Each download unzips into a folder named after its version, so unzipping a newer one
never lands beside an older one or overwrites it.

Nothing needs installing by hand, and no bank card is needed.

Get a free Google API key — the app explains it, and `SETUP-AI.md` is in the folder.

**New in this version**

- **Settings is always reachable.** It now sits outside the scrolling strip, pinned at the end, so
  it cannot be pushed off the edge — which is the section you most want when something is wrong.
- **The strip tells you it scrolls.** It has a visible scrollbar and arrow buttons at each end, and
  the arrows switch off when there is nowhere left to go. Before, it scrolled silently, so it just
  looked like tabs were missing. On a phone the arrows and scrollbar go, since a swipe is the
  natural gesture there.
- **Nothing folds away any more.** Settings, Information, Slides, Statistics and "Since your last
  check" were collapsible cards on a long page; now that each one is its own tab, an extra "open
  me" step is a click too many. They are open, and their headings stay as headings.
- **Slide titles are fixed.** A PDF has no title field, so the title was guessed from the first
  line of each page — and on your export that is a running header, so **every page came out called
  "Candidate name"**. A line repeated down page after page is now recognised as a header and
  skipped, so the real titles come through. On your portfolio that is **56 different titles where
  there was one**.

  **This one needs a check to appear.** The dashboard draws the saved report, and titles are
  worked out when the PDF is parsed, so the old names stay until you press **Check now**. Pages
  that have not changed are not re-sent, so it should cost little or nothing.

**If you are updating:** quit NEA Coach completely first, then open the new copy.

**If you have an older copy already:** quit it first (close the black "NEA Coach server"
window) before starting this one, or the old version will keep answering your browser.

It never modifies your original PowerPoint, and nothing leaves your computer except the
pages a check sends to Google.
