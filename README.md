NEA Coach reviews a Design & Technology NEA portfolio and tells you what to fix, in order of what is costing you marks.

Give it your PowerPoint or a PDF export. It checks the document itself — missing headings, spelling, repetition, empty pages — and then reads every page to judge the work against your mark scheme, with a per-criterion breakdown and a prioritised fix list.

It works with AQA GCSE (8552) and Eduqas A-level. It never modifies your original file, and nothing is uploaded anywhere except the pages a check needs — your portfolio stays on your computer.

Free. You supply your own Google API key; no card, no account, no subscription.
