Add a free AI key

This is the part people find fiddly, so here it is properly. It is free, takes about two minutes, and needs no bank card.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/app/apikey in a new tab and sign in with any Google account.
  2. Click Create API key. If it asks you to pick a project, let it create one.
  3. Copy the key it shows you — it starts with AIza.
  4. Paste it below and press Test this key.
The free tier allows roughly two full checks a day and resets at midnight US Pacific time. The app shows you how much of the day's allowance you have used, as a percentage, so you are never guessing.

    What costs your allowance, and what does not

    Nothing here costs money. Your free Google key comes with a daily allowance, shown by the meter at the bottom of the page. This is what uses it.

    Never uses the allowance — the checks on this page
    Word counts, missing headings, spelling, repetition and empty pages are checked on your own computer, with no internet and no API key. Run them as often as you like.
    A PDF cannot be checked for fonts, spacing or stray boxes, because it does not record them. The app tells you which checks it skipped rather than staying quiet.
    Uses the allowance — the AI review
    Reading your pages and judging the work against the mark scheme. Nothing is sent unless you press Check now.
    A full check is about 9 requests, and the free allowance is roughly 20 a day — so about two full checks a day, and more when little has changed, because untouched pages are never re-sent.
    When the allowance runs out
    A check stops part-way and says so. Nothing is lost — pages already read are kept.
    Quickest fix: paste a different key in Settings. Each key has its own allowance, so a second free key works immediately.
    Or wait: the allowance resets at midnight Pacific time, which is 8am in the UK (7am in summer). The meter says how long is left.
    "Re-check everything"
    Normally only changed pages are sent, so re-checking costs nothing. This button re-reads everything and spends a full check for no new information. Use it only after changing your exam board.
    "Deep thinking" — what it actually does

    The AI takes longer over each page and weighs its judgement before answering. It helps on the calls that need thinking about — whether your evaluation is genuinely critical, whether your development is iterative or just a series of drawings.

    It makes no difference to the obvious things: a missing title or a small font is found either way. It does not change your predicted mark. It is off because it is slower and uses more of the daily allowance. Try it once and compare; if the advice does not read more considered, leave it off.

    How the allowance is made to last
    Untouched pages are never re-sent, which is what makes a second check cheap. The line above the Check button says how many requests a check will take before you press it.
    What the app does, and what it cannot
    It runs two kinds of check. The structural checks measure the document itself — word counts, missing headings, spelling, repetition, empty pages — and cost nothing. The AI review reads the work and judges it against your mark scheme, and spends your daily allowance.
    It does not know your final mark, and it is not the examiner. Treat the predicted mark as a second opinion that is usually within a few marks, and the fix list as the useful part.
    It never writes to your PowerPoint. Your original file is never opened for writing, moved or renamed.
    Where your work and your key are kept
    Everything stays on this computer, in the app's own state folder: your API key in config.json, a copy of your portfolio, the last report, and the history. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except to Google, and only the pages a check actually needs.
    If you give the app to someone else, delete the state folder first. It contains your report and your key. The folder rebuilds itself on first run.
    Changing your API key
    Settings has a Paste a key box. Each key has its own daily allowance, so when one runs out the quickest fix is to paste another — no waiting. Test key checks a key without spending any allowance, because it only asks Google which models the key can use.
    Why a PDF skips some checks
    A PDF records the words on each page but not where anything sits, how large the text is, or any speaker notes. So the checks that measure those cannot run on a PDF, and the app says which it skipped rather than quietly reporting nothing. Supply the .pptx as well to get all of them.
